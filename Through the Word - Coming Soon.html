This Page Has Not Come Off the Bench Yet

You have walked into the study a little early. Nothing is broken and nothing has been lost. This chapter of Through the Word simply has not been written yet.

The whole Bible is being taken one book at a time, one chapter at a time, verse by verse in the King James Bible, and nothing is skipped to make the page count look better. When a chapter's study is finished, its own page will open here in its place. Until then, this page is the sign on the door.

On the Workbench

What Is Being Done With This Chapter

A study here is not a paragraph dashed off in an afternoon. Every chapter is read in full, prayed over, cross referenced, and weighed against the whole counsel of God before a word of it is published.

  • I The text is being read Every verse in its place, in the King James Bible, without a single one passed over.
  • II The witnesses are being gathered Moses and the prophets and the psalms, brought alongside, that the chapter may be seen in the light of Christ.
  • III The study is being written Slowly, and on purpose. Good bread is not hurried, and neither is good doctrine.
  • IV The page will be published When it is finished it will take its own place in the book, and this sign will come down for good.
