The Series

A Full Deep Dive of the Entire Bible

Welcome to Through the Word, a verse by verse journey through the whole of Scripture, from the first verse of Genesis to the last verse of Revelation. This is the full deep dive of the entire Bible by Disciple J.T. May. Nothing is skipped and nothing is rushed. We take the text as it stands in the King James Bible, one book at a time and one chapter at a time, and we let the Word speak for itself.

The whole Bible is one book with one Author and one Saviour at its centre. The Lord Jesus, on the road to Emmaus, opened the Scriptures and showed how Moses, the prophets, and the psalms all spoke of Him. That is the road we are walking here. We read the Old Testament and the New as one continuing story of God and His people, and we hold every chapter up to the light of Christ.

To begin, choose a book below. Each book opens to its own page, where you will find every chapter laid out in order, from the first chapter to the last. Each chapter then opens to a study of its own. The work is being built chapter by chapter over time, so some books and chapters are still on the way. Watch this page grow.
