The Doctrine Library

These are the theological beliefs of Disciple J.T. May gathered in one place grouped by subject so they can be easily found for study. Each teaching stands on the Word of God alone and no private interpretation of the Scriptures. Do not trust the words of this site alone, but test each teaching against the Scriptures to see if they are true. Be a good Berean and search out the Scriptures daily and do not take my word for it. Prove the Word by the Word.
