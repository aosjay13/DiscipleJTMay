Preamble

These Bylaws serve as the foundational declarations of faith, outlining the core biblical tenets and the righteous standard by which believers are called to live. Rooted in Pronomian theology, we affirm that God's standard of righteousness remains unchanging and that His commandments remain a lamp unto our feet.

Article I

The Ten Commandments

We affirm that the Ten Commandments are the foundational laws of God, established for His people. As the eternal and unchanging moral standard, these commandments have not been abolished but remain the binding standard of the Creator for the believer's life.

Article II

The Moedim (Appointed Times)

We believe in the continuous observance of God's established creation order and His appointed times (Moedim). The feast days and appointed seasons were established at the very foundation of the world, codified throughout the Law, and have not been done away with. They remain a perpetual statute and a holy convocation for believers today.

1. The Sabbath — The Seventh Day

The first and foundational appointed time is the Sabbath, set apart by God at creation itself. After six days of work, God rested on the seventh day, blessing it and sanctifying it before any man-made tradition existed. The Sabbath is the seventh day of the week, and it was God who hallowed that specific day. No man has authority to change it to the first day, Sunday, for the LORD declared the seventh day to be His holy day.

And on the seventh day God ended his work which he had made; and he rested on the seventh day from all his work which he had made. Article III

Tithing and the Order of Melchizedek

We believe in the biblical mandate of tithing, established prior to the Levitical priesthood. As Abraham tithed to Melchizedek, we acknowledge that the believer today operates under the eternal priesthood of Jesus Christ, after the order of Melchizedek, and the principle of the tithe remains in effect.

Scriptural References

Genesis 14:20 ...And he gave him tithes of all.
Hebrews 7:17 For he testifieth, Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchisedec.
Article IV

The Holy Ghost

We recognize a distinct, two-fold operation of the Holy Ghost in the life of the believer, as well as the ongoing manifestation of His spiritual gifts for the edification of the body.

Section 1 — The Indwelling and Baptism of the Holy Ghost

At Salvation: Upon believing and receiving salvation, the believer is sealed with and receives the Holy Ghost.

The Baptism of the Holy Ghost: There is a separate, subsequent step wherein the Holy Ghost fully immerses (baptizes) the believer. The biblical evidence of this specific immersion is speaking in tongues.

Section 2 — The Gifts of the Spirit

We firmly affirm that all the spiritual gifts outlined in the Scriptures remain fully operational and active in the body of Christ today. The Holy Ghost distributes these gifts severally as He wills for the profit and edification of the assembly.

The Word of Wisdom: Supernatural insight into the mind, will, and application of God's truth for specific situations or decisions.
The Word of Knowledge: Supernatural revelation of facts, events, or information not known by natural means, given to instruct, warn, or uncover hidden matters.
Faith: A supernatural impartation of unwavering belief and confidence in God for a specific miraculous outcome, distinct from general saving faith.
The Gifts of Healing: Supernatural impartations to cure diseases, sicknesses, and physical ailments without reliance on natural means, demonstrating God's compassion and power.
The Working of Miracles: Divine interventions that alter or supersede natural laws, demonstrating the sovereign power of the Creator.
Prophecy: Speaking forth a message from God by divine inspiration. True biblical prophecy will always perfectly harmonize with God's established written Law and frequently calls His people back to covenant faithfulness and obedience.
Discerning of Spirits: The supernatural ability to perceive the source of a spiritual manifestation, distinguishing whether it originates from the Holy Spirit, demonic entities, or the human spirit.
Divers Kinds of Tongues: Supernatural utterances in languages never learned by the speaker, utilized for personal edification or, when interpreted, for the edification of the assembly.
The Interpretation of Tongues: The supernatural ability to translate or reveal the meaning of an utterance spoken in an unknown tongue, so that the entire assembly may understand and be edified.

Article V

Water Baptism

We affirm that water baptism is a work and is therefore not a requirement for salvation, as salvation is by grace through faith alone. However, while it does not save, it is a righteous work of obedience that a believer's heart should naturally desire immediately upon being saved.

Scriptural References

Ephesians 2:8–9 For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: not of works, lest any man should boast.
1 Corinthians 1:14, 17 I thank God that I baptized none of you, but Crispus and Gaius... For Christ sent me not to baptize, but to preach the gospel...
Acts 8:36–38 And as they went on their way, they came unto a certain water: and the eunuch said, See, here is water; what doth hinder me to be baptized? And Philip said, If thou believest with all thine heart, thou mayest. And he answered and said, I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God. And he commanded the chariot to stand still: and they went down both into the water, both Philip and the eunuch; and he baptized him.
Article VI

Sanctification and Justification

We believe that true biblical faith inherently produces obedience. Following salvation, the believer must deeply desire to follow God's Commandments. Sanctification requires us to be doers of the word and not hearers only, recognizing that faith void of obedient works to God's law is dead.

Scriptural References

James 1:22 But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.
James 2:20 But wilt thou know, O vain man, that faith without works is dead?
1 John 5:3 For this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not grievous.
Article VII

Backsliding

We define backsliding as turning away from God's Commandments to pursue one's own will. Such departure requires immediate repentance. The state of a person's soul at the end of their life is fixed; therefore, it is imperative to remain steadfast, for where a tree falls, so shall it lie.

Scriptural References

2 Peter 2:21 For it had been better for them not to have known the way of righteousness, than, after they have known it, to turn from the holy commandment delivered unto them.
Ecclesiastes 11:3 ...and if the tree fall toward the south, or toward the north, in the place where the tree falleth, there it shall be.
Article VIII

Repentance

We define repentance not merely as a singular confession, but as the continual state of living free from sin. Because sin is the transgression of God's Law, true repentance is a sustained return to observing His commandments and walking in His ways.

Scriptural References

1 John 3:4 Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.
Romans 6:1–2 What shall we say then? Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound? God forbid...
Article IX

The Fivefold Ministry

We recognize and affirm the continuous operation of the fivefold ministry gifts within the body of Christ. God has explicitly appointed apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, and teachers for the perfecting of the saints, the work of the ministry, and the edifying of the assembly. These spiritual offices work together in harmony to bring the body to the unity of the faith and maturity in Christ, establishing His people in sound biblical doctrine.

As Paul writes in 1 Timothy 2:11–15, a woman is not to teach or usurp authority over the man, for Adam was formed first, and it was the woman who was deceived and fell into transgression. Therefore, anyone who holds any of these offices must be a man.

Section 1 — Apostles

Apostles are "sent ones" commissioned by God to be foundational builders and overseers within the body of Christ. They establish assemblies, maintain doctrinal purity by ensuring alignment with the whole counsel of God (including His Law), and impart spiritual guidance and order, operating with a high level of spiritual authority and spiritual fatherhood.

Section 2 — Prophets

Prophets speak by divine inspiration to bring edification, exhortation, and comfort to the body. A true biblical prophet will always align with the established written Word of God and will often carry the mandate to call God's people to repentance, urging a return to covenant faithfulness and obedience to His commandments. Prophets hold the Government of God in their heart, mind, soul, and strength.

Section 3 — Evangelists

Evangelists are uniquely gifted and burdened to proclaim the Gospel of the Kingdom to the lost. They call individuals to repent of their sin, which is the transgression of the Law, and to turn to faith in Jesus Christ. Their ministry is focused on gathering the harvest and bringing new believers into the fold.

Section 4 — Pastors

Pastors (Shepherds) are called to lead, feed, and protect the local assembly. They provide ongoing spiritual care, guiding the flock in the paths of righteousness, guarding against false teachings and grievous wolves, and fostering a spiritually healthy community of believers who walk in God's ways.

Section 5 — Teachers

Teachers are given a special grace to expound upon the Scriptures, bringing deep clarity and understanding of God's Word from Genesis to Revelation. They instruct the body in sound doctrine and the practical application of God's commandments, ensuring the assembly is firmly rooted and grounded in biblical truth.

Scriptural References

1 Timothy 2:11–15 But I suffer not a woman to teach, nor to usurp authority over the man, but to be in silence. For Adam was first formed, then Eve. And Adam was not deceived, but the woman being deceived was in the transgression. Notwithstanding she shall be saved in childbearing, if they continue in faith and charity and holiness with sobriety.
Ephesians 4:11–13 And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers; for the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ: till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ.

1 Corinthians 14:3 But he that prophesieth speaketh unto men to edification, and exhortation, and comfort.
Article X

Qualifications for Leadership

Leadership within the assembly is strictly governed by biblical qualifications. The requirement to be the "husband of one wife" explicitly prohibits polygamy; it is not a restriction regarding historical divorce and scriptural remarriage.

Section 1 — Qualifications of an Elder (Bishop/Pastor)

The Elder is called to teach, lead, and oversee the spiritual health of the assembly. As 1 Timothy 3:1–7 and Titus 1:5–9 establish, an elder must be:

  • A man
  • Blameless
  • The husband of one wife (strictly non-polygamous)
  • Vigilant, sober, of good behaviour
  • Given to hospitality, apt to teach
  • Not given to wine, no striker
  • Not greedy of filthy lucre
  • Patient, not a brawler, not covetous
  • One that ruleth well his own house
  • Having his children in subjection with all gravity
  • Not a novice (new convert)
  • A good report of them which are without

Section 2 — Qualifications of a Deacon

The Deacon is called to serve the practical, physical, and administrative needs of the assembly. As 1 Timothy 3:8–13 establishes, a deacon must be:

  • A man
  • Grave, not double-tongued
  • Not given to much wine
  • Not greedy of filthy lucre
  • Holding the mystery of the faith in a pure conscience
  • First proved; then found blameless
  • The husband of one wife (strictly non-polygamous)
  • Ruling their children and their own houses well

Scriptural References

1 Timothy 3:1–7 This is a true saying, if a man desire the office of a bishop, he desireth a good work. A bishop then must be blameless, the husband of one wife, vigilant, sober, of good behaviour, given to hospitality, apt to teach; not given to wine, no striker, not greedy of filthy lucre; but patient, not a brawler, not covetous; one that ruleth well his own house, having his children in subjection with all gravity; (for if a man know not how to rule his own house, how shall he take care of the church of God?) Not a novice, lest being lifted up with pride he fall into the condemnation of the devil. Moreover he must have a good report of them which are without; lest he fall into reproach and the snare of the devil.
Titus 1:5–9 For this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting, and ordain elders in every city, as I had appointed thee: if any be blameless, the husband of one wife, having faithful children not accused of riot or unruly. For a bishop must be blameless, as the steward of God; not selfwilled, not soon angry, not given to wine, no striker, not given to filthy lucre; but a lover of hospitality, a lover of good men, sober, just, holy, temperate; holding fast the faithful word as he hath been taught, that he may be able by sound doctrine both to exhort and to convince the gainsayers.
1 Timothy 3:8–13 Likewise must the deacons be grave, not doubletongued, not given to much wine, not greedy of filthy lucre; holding the mystery of the faith in a pure conscience. And let these also first be proved; then let them use the office of a deacon, being found blameless. Even so must their wives be grave, not slanderers, sober, faithful in all things. Let the deacons be the husbands of one wife, ruling their children and their own houses well. For they that have used the office of a deacon well purchase to themselves a good degree, and great boldness in the faith which is in Christ Jesus.
Article XI

The Chronological Harmony of the End Times

A Scriptural Alignment of Matthew 24, Luke 21, Revelation 6, the Prophets, and the Epistles.

The most profound key to understanding the end-time timeline is recognizing that Christ's teaching in the Olivet Discourse (recorded in both Matthew 24 and Luke 21) is exactly the same chronological sequence as the opening of the Seals in Revelation 6. When we layer the Old Testament prophets and Paul's epistles on top of this dual-Gospel witness, a perfect, unbreakable Post-Tribulation / Pre-Wrath timeline emerges.

Article XII

Final Authority of the Scriptures

We affirm that the entirety of the Bible, from Genesis to Revelation, is the complete, infallible, inspired, and authoritative Word of God. It is the supreme and final authority for our faith, our doctrine, and our daily practice. We strictly reject the traditions of men, worldly philosophies, and man-made religious dogmas that attempt to alter, contradict, or make void the commandments of God. We commit to living by the whole counsel of Scripture, guided by the Holy Ghost, in steadfast obedience to our Creator.

Scriptural References

2 Timothy 3:16–17 All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness: that the man of God may be perfect, thoroughly furnished unto all good works.
Mark 7:8 For laying aside the commandment of God, ye hold the tradition of men...

Colossians 2:8 Beware lest any man spoil you through philosophy and vain deceit, after the tradition of men, after the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ.
Article XIII

Warning Against False Doctrine and Deception

We solemnly warn the assembly to remain eternally vigilant against the rise of false doctrine and those who would pervert the true Gospel of the Kingdom. The Scriptures explicitly prophesy that in the latter times, some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits and doctrines of devils.

We stand fiercely on guard against ravenous wolves in sheep's clothing who will rise up from within and without to deceive many, speaking perverse things to draw away disciples after themselves. Furthermore, we wholly reject the ministries of false apostles and deceitful workers, apostles of Satan who transform themselves into angels of light. Believers must be firmly rooted in the Law and the Prophets, resisting the temptation to seek out teachers who merely satisfy itching ears with worldly fables and smooth words that make void the commandments of God.

2 Corinthians 11:13–15 For such are false apostles, deceitful workers, transforming themselves into the apostles of Christ. And no marvel; for Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light...
Matthew 24:11 And many false prophets shall rise, and shall deceive many.
