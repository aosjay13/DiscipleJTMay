Welcome

A Place to Study the Scriptures

Welcome. My name is Brother Justin May, and I teach the Bible. The teachings gathered here are Pronomian in theology. That word simply means I hold that the Law of God still stands as the shape of a faithful life, while salvation itself is the free gift of God, given by grace through faith in Jesus Christ.

We are saved by grace, not by keeping the Law. Yet a heart that loves God keeps His commandments. The moral commandments, the seventh-day Sabbath, and the appointed feasts still belong to the believer, while the priesthood and the sacrifices found their end in Christ. Obedience here is not a burden. It is the fruit of love.

Read slowly, compare everything to Scripture, and hold fast to what is good.

Isaiah 8:20 To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.
