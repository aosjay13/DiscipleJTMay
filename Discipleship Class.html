Grow in the Faith

A Class for Making Disciples

This is a step by step class for the new believer and for anyone who wants to be grounded in the faith. We move level by level and lesson by lesson, building on the plain witness of the King James Bible. Some lessons are spread over several parts, a class lecture, a set of study questions, a wide collection of supporting Scriptures, and a homework worksheet. Others are gathered into a single document that carries the lesson, the questions, and the answer key together.

Take your time, read slowly, and look up every verse for yourself.
