Am I Truly Born Again?

A Personal Self-Examination Before God

This is not a test with right and wrong answers to impress your teacher. This is an honest, private evaluation between you and God. Answer every question truthfully. The goal is not to feel good about yourself, but to see yourself clearly in the light of Scripture, so that if anything needs to change, you can act on it today.
I The Test of Examination

Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith; prove your own selves. Know ye not your own selves, how that Jesus Christ is in you, except ye be reprobates?

2 Corinthians 13:5

Paul commands every believer to examine themselves. Before you answer anything else on this worksheet, sit quietly for a moment, then answer honestly.

1a. In your own words, what do you believe it means to be "born again"? Write your answer before reading further.

1b. Now read the passage below. How does your answer above compare to what Jesus actually said?

Jesus answered and said unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God... Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God. That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit. Marvel not that I said unto thee, Ye must be born again.

John 3:3, 5–7
II The Test of Obedience

And hereby we do know that we know him, if we keep his commandments. He that saith, I know him, and keepeth not his commandments, is a liar, and the truth is not in him.

1 John 2:3–4

Whosoever is born of God doth not commit sin; for his seed remaineth in him: and he cannot sin, because he is born of God.

1 John 3:9

For this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not grievous.

1 John 5:3

2a. Scripture says the born-again believer does not continue in a lifestyle of sin (law-breaking). How would you honestly describe your current relationship with God's commandments? Circle one:

I rarely think about them
I know them but struggle to follow them
I desire them and am growing
They are a delight to me

2b. Is there a specific commandment you know you are currently breaking or avoiding? Write it here (this is between you and God).

2c. Do God's commandments feel like a burden or a love? Explain why you feel that way.

III The Test of a New Heart

A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh. And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.

Ezekiel 36:26–27

When God truly transforms a person, the result is not just forgiveness. It is a new desire to walk in His statutes; the Spirit literally causes the regenerate person to want to obey.

3a. Can you point to a real, noticeable change in your desires since you received Christ? Describe it specifically.

3b. Before salvation, did sin bother you the way it does now? How has your attitude toward sin changed, or has it not changed much? Be honest.

IV The Test of Fruit

Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles? Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit; but a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit... Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven.

Matthew 7:16–17, 21

But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.

Galatians 5:22–23

4a. Check every fruit below that people who know you well would genuinely say describes your life right now. Be honest, not hopeful.

Love for others, including difficult people
Joy not based merely on circumstances
Peace even in hard seasons
Patience (longsuffering) with people
Gentleness in how I speak and act
Goodness (doing right when no one watches)
Faith that shows in my daily choices
Meekness (strength under control)
Temperance (self-control over my appetites)

4b. Looking at the ones you did not check, which area is the Holy Spirit calling you to grow in most urgently? Why?

V The Test of Love for the Brethren

We know that we have passed from death unto life, because we love the brethren. He that loveth not his brother abideth in death.

1 John 3:14

He that saith he is in the light, and hateth his brother, is in darkness even until now.

1 John 2:9

5a. Scripture gives us one of the clearest signs of regeneration here: genuine love for fellow believers. Is there currently anyone in the body of Christ toward whom you carry bitterness, hatred, or unforgiveness? (Yes or No)

5b. If yes, what specific step will you take this week to address it, based on what the Word says?

VI The Test of Faith and Works Together

What doth it profit, my brethren, though a man say he hath faith, and have not works? can faith save him?... Even so faith, if it hath not works, is dead, being alone.

James 2:14, 17

Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus.

Revelation 14:12

6a. The saints of God are defined by two things together: the faith of Jesus AND keeping the commandments. In your own life, would you say both of these are truly present, or does one exist without the other? Write your honest assessment.

A Sober Warning from Scripture

"Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven. Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name?... And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity."

Matthew 7:21–23

VII The Final Honest Assessment

7a. Based on everything you worked through above, honestly answer: Do you believe you are truly born again? Explain your answer using evidence from your own life, not just a prayer you prayed in the past.

7b. If after this evaluation you are unsure whether you are truly saved, what does the following verse tell you to do right now?

But if the wicked will turn from all his sins that he hath committed, and keep all my statutes, and do that which is lawful and right, he shall surely live, he shall not die.

Ezekiel 18:21

7c. Write out a short, specific, honest prayer to God below. Not a performance, just the truth of where you are with Him today.

I have completed this self-examination honestly and before God, not for the eyes of others.

Lesson 1 Homework Supplement

Answer & Pastoral Guide

Because this worksheet is a subjective self-evaluation, there is no traditional "right/wrong" answer key for most questions. Use this guide to review student responses, gauge their spiritual condition, and know how to counsel them.

Section I: The Test of Examination (1a & 1b)
Look for the student to recognize the difference between their personal definition (1a) and Jesus' definition (1b). Counseling Note: Jesus defined it as a spiritual rebirth by water and the Spirit resulting in a changed nature, not merely praying a prayer or going to church. Ensure the student grasps this foundational difference.
Section II: The Test of Obedience (2a, 2b, & 2c)
True believers delight in God's commands (1 John 5:3 says they are "not grievous"). Struggle is normal, but apathy or rebellion is a red flag. Red Flag: If the student circles "I rarely think about them" (2a) or describes God's commandments as a heavy burden (2c), they may still have a "stony heart" or lack proper teaching on grace-fueled obedience. Use this to open a dialogue.
Section III: The Test of a New Heart (3a & 3b)
A genuinely converted person will be able to articulate a shift from loving sin to hating it, fulfilling Ezekiel 36. Counseling Note: Look for evidence of godly sorrow and conviction of the Holy Spirit. If they state that their attitude toward sin has "not changed much," they need to hear the Gospel of repentance again.
Section IV: The Test of Fruit (4a & 4b)
This is a personal assessment of the Fruit of the Spirit. No one is perfect, but the trajectory of their life should display these traits. Counseling Note: Use question 4b to encourage them. Ask them how you can pray for them specifically regarding the fruit they identified as lacking.
Section V: The Test of Love for the Brethren (5a & 5b)
1 John states emphatically that he who hates his brother is in darkness. Red Flag: If they answer "Yes" to holding bitterness or unforgiveness, this is a critical discipleship moment. Direct them to Matthew 18 and Matthew 6:14-15. Unforgiveness is a severe spiritual blockage.
Section VI: The Test of Faith and Works Together (6a)
Look for an acknowledgment that biblical faith naturally produces obedience/works. Counseling Note: Ensure they see faith and obedience as partners in the life of a saint (Revelation 14:12), not as enemies or contradictory concepts.
Section VII: Final Assessment (7a & 7b)
Explicit Answer for 7b: According to Ezekiel 18:21, they must turn from all their sins, keep God's statutes, and do what is lawful and right. This is the biblical definition of repentance. Counseling Note: If their honest assessment in 7a is "No" or "I'm not sure," gently guide them through repentance using Ezekiel 18 and pray with them. Do not offer false assurance; let the Word of God give them true assurance as they align with it.
