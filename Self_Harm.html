Self Harm

Why the Body of Christ Will Not Wound Itself

✦

Brother Justin May · Work the Word Church, Madison

The Main Text

Ephesians 5:29–30For no man ever yet hated his own flesh; but nourisheth and cherisheth it, even as the Lord the church: For we are members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones.
1 Corinthians 12:25–26That there should be no schism in the body; but that the members should have the same care one for another. And whether one member suffer, all the members suffer with it; or one member be honoured, all the members rejoice with it.

The Big Idea

One Sentence to Carry Home

Your body never turns on itself, because every member takes its orders from the same mind. When you are born again, God puts the mind of Christ in you, and the mind of Christ will never tell one member of His body to wound another.

A Word About the Title

The title of this message is a picture. We are talking about a church that wounds its own members. But someone reading or hearing this may be hurting themselves in the most literal way. If that is you, God has not given up on you or on your body. Stay with this message to the end, because there is a word in the appeal written just for you.

Introduction

No Man Ever Yet Hated His Own Flesh

Fearfully and Wonderfully Made

Psalm 139:14I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well.

Before we talk about the body of Christ, look at the body God gave you. Toes, feet, legs, fingers, hands, arms, a mouth, eyes, ears, and a mind to direct them all. Not one of those members works alone. Every one of them was built to serve the others.

Paul says no man ever yet hated his own flesh (Ephesians 5:29). Think about it. If you smash your thumb with a hammer, you do not take the hammer to the hand that missed. The whole body runs to help the thumb. You grab it, hold it, and protect it until it heals. That is how Christ treats His church, and that is how the church is supposed to treat itself.

Many Members, One Body

Romans 12:4–5For as we have many members in one body, and all members have not the same office: So we, being many, are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another.

Every one members one of another. You do not only belong to Christ. You belong to each other. My hand is not a separate thing from my foot. They are both me.

Bite and Devour

Galatians 5:14–15For all the law is fulfilled in one word, even in this; Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself. But if ye bite and devour one another, take heed that ye be not consumed one of another.

Here is the danger. Paul warns of a church that bites and devours itself until there is nothing left. That is self harm in the body of Christ: members turning on members. A body can survive a lot of attacks from the outside. What it cannot survive is attacking itself.

I · 1 Corinthians 12

One Body, Many Members

Paul already told this parable. Before we walk through the members one at a time, hear how he told it.

So Also Is Christ

1 Corinthians 12:12–14For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of that one body, being many, are one body: so also is Christ. For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free; and have been all made to drink into one Spirit. For the body is not one member, but many.

So also is Christ. Paul does not say "so also is the church." He says Christ. The church is His body. And notice how you get in: by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body. God is the one who places you in it.

As It Hath Pleased Him

1 Corinthians 12:15–20If the foot shall say, Because I am not the hand, I am not of the body; is it therefore not of the body? And if the ear shall say, Because I am not the eye, I am not of the body; is it therefore not of the body? If the whole body were an eye, where were the hearing? If the whole were hearing, where were the smelling? But now hath God set the members every one of them in the body, as it hath pleased him. And if they were all one member, where were the body? But now are they many members, yet but one body.

The first problem is a member that looks down on itself. The foot says, "I am not the hand, so I must not belong." Then Paul asks what would happen if the whole body were an eye. You would see everything and hear nothing. God set every member right where He wanted it (1 Corinthians 12:18).

I Have No Need of Thee

1 Corinthians 12:21–24And the eye cannot say unto the hand, I have no need of thee: nor again the head to the feet, I have no need of you. Nay, much more those members of the body, which seem to be more feeble, are necessary: And those members of the body, which we think to be less honourable, upon these we bestow more abundant honour; and our uncomely parts have more abundant comeliness. For our comely parts have no need: but God hath tempered the body together, having given more abundant honour to that part which lacked:

The second problem is the opposite. The eye looks at the hand and says, "I do not need you." That is pride. Paul says the members that seem to be more feeble are necessary. Not nice to have. Necessary.

The Same Care One for Another

1 Corinthians 12:25–27That there should be no schism in the body; but that the members should have the same care one for another. And whether one member suffer, all the members suffer with it; or one member be honoured, all the members rejoice with it. Now ye are the body of Christ, and members in particular.

This is what God wants for His body: no schism, no tearing. The members have the same care one for another. When one hurts, everybody hurts. When one is honoured, everybody rejoices. A healthy body does not compete with itself.

Notice the Members Paul Named

Paul names the foot, the hand, the ear, the eye, and the head. In this message we take his parable and walk the whole body, from the toes up to the mind.

II · The Parable of Your Body

The Members That Stand and Walk

We start at the ground. These members hold the rest of the body up and carry it wherever it needs to go.

1

The Toes

Balance

Thumbs and Great Toes

Judges 1:6–7But Adonibezek fled; and they pursued after him, and caught him, and cut off his thumbs and his great toes. And Adonibezek said, Threescore and ten kings, having their thumbs and their great toes cut off, gathered their meat under my table: as I have done, so God hath requited me. And they brought him to Jerusalem, and there he died.

When Israel caught Adonibezek, they cut off his thumbs and his great toes, the same thing he had done to seventy kings. Why the toes? A man without his great toes cannot keep his balance, cannot run, and cannot stand firm in a fight. The enemy knows that if he can take out the smallest members, the whole body is crippled.

✦ Bonus: Blood on the Ear, the Thumb, and the Great Toe

The Priests Set Apart

Leviticus 8:23–24And he slew it; and Moses took of the blood of it, and put it upon the tip of Aaron's right ear, and upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the great toe of his right foot. And he brought Aaron's sons, and Moses put of the blood upon the tip of their right ear, and upon the thumbs of their right hands, and upon the great toes of their right feet: and Moses sprinkled the blood upon the altar round about.

When Aaron and his sons were set apart as priests, Moses put the blood on the right ear, the right thumb, and the right great toe. Their hearing, their working, and their walking were all set apart for God, down to the smallest toe. Now God calls His people a royal priesthood (1 Peter 2:9). No member of you is too small to belong to Him.

What They Do

Toes keep you balanced, push off with every step, and grip the ground so you do not fall.

How They Help the Others

They let the legs carry weight and the feet move forward without the whole body tipping over. You never think about your toes until you stub one, and then the whole body stops.

In the Body of Christ

The hidden members. The ones who pray in the back, clean up after service, and keep showing up. Nobody sees them, but take them away and the church loses its balance (1 Corinthians 12:22).

2

The Feet

Going

How Beautiful Are the Feet

Romans 10:15And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!

The mouth preaches, but the feet have to get the preacher there. God calls those feet beautiful.

Shod With the Gospel

Ephesians 6:15And your feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace;

In the armour of God, the feet are made ready with the gospel of peace. Feet that carry peace, not trouble.

Wash One Another's Feet

John 13:14If I then, your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; ye also ought to wash one another's feet.

The feet get the dirtiest because they go the farthest. Jesus did not despise dirty feet. He washed them, and He told us to do the same for each other.

What They Do

Feet carry the whole body wherever it needs to go.

How They Help the Others

The eyes can see a need, but the feet take the hands there to meet it. Feet take the most pounding so the rest of the body can arrive.

In the Body of Christ

The ones who go. They visit the sick, knock on doors, give someone a ride to church, and carry the gospel where it is needed.

When They Turn on the Body

God hates feet that be swift in running to mischief (Proverbs 6:18). Carrying a story from house to house is still walking.

3

The Legs

Strength

Lift Up the Feeble Knees

Hebrews 12:12–13Wherefore lift up the hands which hang down, and the feeble knees; And make straight paths for your feet, lest that which is lame be turned out of the way; but let it rather be healed.

Look at what God says to do with a weak or lame member. Do not turn it out of the way. Heal it. Your body never cuts off a sprained ankle. It holds it still, shifts the weight to the other leg, and lets it heal.

Bear Ye One Another's Burdens

Galatians 6:2Bear ye one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.

Legs bear the weight. That is their whole job. When one member cannot carry the load, the strong ones carry it for them.

Having Done All, to Stand

Ephesians 6:13Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand.

Legs keep you standing when the evil day comes.

What They Do

The legs hold up the weight of the whole body. They let you stand, climb, and carry a load.

How They Help the Others

When the legs bear the weight, the hands are free to work. When one leg is hurt, the other one takes more of the weight until it heals.

In the Body of Christ

The steady and strong who carry burdens and hold the house up in hard seasons. They stand when others are shaking.

III · The Parable of Your Body

The Members That Work and Lift

Next come the members that do the work. They feed the body, tend its wounds, and lift its load.

4

The Fingers

Skill

My Fingers to Fight

Psalm 144:1Blessed be the LORD my strength, which teacheth my hands to war, and my fingers to fight:

God trains the fingers as well as the hands. The fine, careful work of the body is His work too.

The Putting Forth of the Finger

Isaiah 58:9Then shalt thou call, and the LORD shall answer; thou shalt cry, and he shall say, Here I am. If thou take away from the midst of thee the yoke, the putting forth of the finger, and speaking vanity;

Here is the warning. God told Israel to take away the putting forth of the finger, the pointing and the accusing. Fingers were made to build and to mend. When they are used to point at a brother, they wound the body.

What They Do

Fingers do the careful work. They button a shirt, turn a page, play an instrument, and pull out a splinter.

How They Help the Others

Without fingers the hand is only a fist. They give the hand its skill, and they are the first thing to reach a hurting place on the body.

In the Body of Christ

The skilled and detailed servants: musicians, builders and fixers, and those who handle the small careful tasks that keep everything running.

When They Turn on the Body

A pointed finger. Blame and accusation where there should have been help.

5

The Hands

Work

Whatsoever Thy Hand Findeth

Ecclesiastes 9:10Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might; for there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest.

The hand is the worker of the body. Whatever it finds to do, it does with all its might.

That He May Have to Give

Ephesians 4:28Let him that stole steal no more: but rather let him labour, working with his hands the thing which is good, that he may have to give to him that needeth.

Look at why the hands work: that he may have to give to him that needeth. Hands do not work only for themselves. They work so they can give to another member.

Lay Hands on the Sick

Mark 16:18They shall take up serpents; and if they drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them; they shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall recover.

Believers lay hands on the sick, and God heals them. In the body of Christ, hands are healing hands.

What They Do

Hands work, build, carry, and hold.

How They Help the Others

The hand feeds the mouth, washes the face, rubs a sore muscle, and bandages the knee. It serves every other member, and it never asks the foot what it has done for it lately.

In the Body of Christ

The workers and servants of the church. The ones who serve, cook, give, and lay hands on the sick.

When They Turn on the Body

Hands that shed innocent blood are on the list of things God hates (Proverbs 6:17). The same hand that bandages can also strike.

6

The Arms

Lifting

Aaron and Hur Stayed Up His Hands

Exodus 17:11–12And it came to pass, when Moses held up his hand, that Israel prevailed: and when he let down his hand, Amalek prevailed. But Moses' hands were heavy; and they took a stone, and put it under him, and he sat thereon; and Aaron and Hur stayed up his hands, the one on the one side, and the other on the other side; and his hands were steady until the going down of the sun.

When Moses got tired, Israel started losing. Aaron and Hur did not criticize Moses for being tired. They stood on each side and held his arms up until the sun went down. That is what arms do in the body.

He Shall Gather the Lambs With His Arm

Isaiah 40:11He shall feed his flock like a shepherd: he shall gather the lambs with his arm, and carry them in his bosom, and shall gently lead those that are with young.

The Shepherd gathers the lambs with His arm and carries them close. Arms embrace the weak and the young.

The Everlasting Arms

Deuteronomy 33:27The eternal God is thy refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms: and he shall thrust out the enemy from before thee; and shall say, Destroy them.

Underneath everything are God's everlasting arms. Our arms are meant to show people what His arms are like.

What They Do

Arms reach, lift, carry, and embrace.

How They Help the Others

When you trip, your arms fly out before you can think, to protect your head and face. Arms will take a hit to keep another member from getting hurt.

In the Body of Christ

Those who lift up the weary, hold up the arms of their leaders, embrace the backslider coming home, and carry the young in the faith.

IV · The Parable of Your Body

The Members of the Head

Now we come up to the head. The mouth, the eyes, and the ears sit closest to the mind, and they have the most power to help the body or to hurt it.

7

The Mouth

Nourishing and Speaking

Death and Life

Proverbs 18:21Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof.

The mouth holds the power of death and life.

It Defileth the Whole Body

James 3:5–6Even so the tongue is a little member, and boasteth great things. Behold, how great a matter a little fire kindleth! And the tongue is a fire, a world of iniquity: so is the tongue among our members, that it defileth the whole body, and setteth on fire the course of nature; and it is set on fire of hell.

James calls the tongue a little member, but it defileth the whole body. One small member can set the whole body on fire. More churches have been burned down by tongues than by matches.

That It May Minister Grace

Ephesians 4:29Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers.

Here is the test for every word. Does it build up, and does it minister grace to the one hearing it?

What It Does

The mouth takes in food, and it speaks for the body.

How It Helps the Others

Every member is fed through the mouth. Even the toes are nourished by what the mouth takes in. If the mouth stops eating, the whole body starves. If it takes in poison, the whole body gets sick.

In the Body of Christ

Our words feed the church or poison it: preaching, teaching, encouraging, praying, and confessing Christ (Romans 10:9–10).

When It Turns on the Body

Gossip, backbiting, and complaining. The tongue defileth the whole body (James 3:6).

8

The Eyes

Sight

The Light of the Body

Matthew 6:22–23The light of the body is the eye: if therefore thine eye be single, thy whole body shall be full of light. But if thine eye be evil, thy whole body shall be full of darkness. If therefore the light that is in thee be darkness, how great is that darkness!

The eye is the light of the whole body. What the eye takes in fills the whole body with light or with darkness.

Eyes to the Blind

Job 29:15I was eyes to the blind, and feet was I to the lame.

Job was eyes to the blind and feet to the lame. That is the body of Christ. When one member is missing something, another member supplies it.

The Mote and the Beam

Matthew 7:3–5And why beholdest thou the mote that is in thy brother's eye, but considerest not the beam that is in thine own eye? Or how wilt thou say to thy brother, Let me pull out the mote out of thine eye; and, behold, a beam is in thine own eye? Thou hypocrite, first cast out the beam out of thine own eye; and then shalt thou see clearly to cast out the mote out of thy brother's eye.

But the eye can be turned on the body too. It can go hunting for a speck in a brother's eye and ignore the beam in its own. Jesus did not say never help your brother with his speck. He said deal with your own beam first, and then you will see clearly enough to help.

What They Do

Eyes see the road ahead and give light to the whole body.

How They Help the Others

The eyes do not walk, but they keep the feet out of the hole. They guide the hands to their work, and they see danger before the body gets there.

In the Body of Christ

The watchmen and those with discernment. They see danger coming and warn the body, and they notice the needs of others.

When They Turn on the Body

A critical eye that looks for faults instead of needs.

9

The Ears

Hearing and Balance

Faith Cometh by Hearing

Romans 10:17So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.

Faith comes into the body through the ears, by hearing the word of God.

Swift to Hear

James 1:19Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath:

God gave you two ears and one mouth. Be quick to listen and slow to speak.

A Liar Giveth Ear

Proverbs 17:4A wicked doer giveth heed to false lips; and a liar giveth ear to a naughty tongue.

Gossip cannot travel without a listening ear. A tongue needs an ear to do its damage, and when the ears refuse to listen to a naughty tongue, the fire goes out.

What They Do

Ears hear, and deep inside the ear sits the body's sense of balance.

How They Help the Others

The ears hear what the eyes cannot see, like danger coming from behind. And when the inner ear gets out of order, the whole body gets dizzy and cannot walk straight.

In the Body of Christ

Those who hear the Word and keep the church balanced. Hearing the word of God is what keeps the body steady.

When They Turn on the Body

Ears that welcome gossip and false teaching.

V · The Parable of Your Body

The Mind: Where Every Member Takes Its Orders

Every member we just looked at has one thing in common. None of them moves on its own. Your hand does not decide to pick up a cup. Your mind tells it to. Every member takes its orders from the head.

10

The Mind

Direction

He Is the Head of the Body

Colossians 1:18And he is the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead; that in all things he might have the preeminence.

Christ is the head of the body, the church. He has the preeminence, and He gives the orders.

Not Holding the Head

Colossians 2:19And not holding the Head, from which all the body by joints and bands having nourishment ministered, and knit together, increaseth with the increase of God.

When the body holds on to the Head, it is knit together and it increaseth with the increase of God. The joints and bands pass nourishment from member to member. A body that lets go of the Head comes apart.

As He Thinketh

Proverbs 23:7For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he: Eat and drink, saith he to thee; but his heart is not with thee.

What a man is on the outside follows what he thinks on the inside. The members follow the mind.

The Carnal Mind

Romans 8:6–7For to be carnally minded is death; but to be spiritually minded is life and peace. Because the carnal mind is enmity against God: for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be.

There are two minds a body can follow. The carnal mind brings death, fights against God, and will not submit to His law. The spiritual mind brings life and peace.

What It Does

The mind sends signals through the nerves to every member of the body.

How It Helps the Others

The mind coordinates everything. It tells the eyes to look, the feet to step, and the hands to catch. When one member gets hurt, the mind sends every other member to help.

In the Body of Christ

Christ is the Head, and every member is meant to take its orders from Him.

When the Signal Goes Wrong

If the signal from the head is cut, the member goes limp. That is paralysis. If the signal gets confused, the members start working against each other. The problem with a member that is hurting the body is almost never the member. It is the mind that member is taking orders from.

VI · Self Harm

When the Members Turn on the Body

Now we come to the title of this message.

Seven Things the LORD Hates

Proverbs 6:16–19These six things doth the LORD hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him: A proud look, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood, An heart that deviseth wicked imaginations, feet that be swift in running to mischief, A false witness that speaketh lies, and he that soweth discord among brethren.

Look closely at that list. Nearly every item is a member of the body. Every member we praised in this message can be turned into a weapon against the body.

The MemberTurned Against the Body
The EyesA proud look
The MouthA lying tongue
The HandsHands that shed innocent blood
The MindAn heart that deviseth wicked imaginations
The FeetFeet that be swift in running to mischief
The Mouth AgainA false witness that speaketh lies
The Whole BodyHe that soweth discord among brethren

The last one sums up all the rest. He that soweth discord among brethren is a member turning the whole body against itself.

Lusts That War in Your Members

James 4:1From whence come wars and fightings among you? come they not hence, even of your lusts that war in your members?

Where do the fights in a church come from? James says from lusts that war in your members. The war starts inside one person before it ever spreads to the body.

Another Law in My Members

Romans 7:22–23For I delight in the law of God after the inward man: But I see another law in my members, warring against the law of my mind, and bringing me into captivity to the law of sin which is in my members.

Paul felt it too. His inward man delighted in God's law, but another law in his members kept warring against his mind. A member that takes orders from the flesh instead of the mind will always fight.

Divided Against Itself

Matthew 12:25And Jesus knew their thoughts, and said unto them, Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation; and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand:

Jesus said it plainly. A house divided against itself will not stand. The enemy does not have to destroy a church that is willing to destroy itself.

When the Body Attacks Itself

Doctors have a name for it when the body's own defenses attack its healthy parts: an autoimmune disease. The very system God built to fight off invaders gets confused and turns on the body. That is what happens when a church takes the discernment and correction meant for the enemy and aims it at its own brothers and sisters. If ye bite and devour one another, take heed that ye be not consumed one of another (Galatians 5:15).

VII · Born Again

The Mind of Christ Placed in the Body

So how does a body stop harming itself? It does not get new members. It gets a new mind.

A.Ye Must Be Born Again

Born of the Spirit

John 3:3–7Jesus answered and said unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God. Nicodemus saith unto him, How can a man be born when he is old? can he enter the second time into his mother's womb, and be born? Jesus answered, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God. That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit. Marvel not that I said unto thee, Ye must be born again.

Nobody gets into the kingdom by improving the old life. You have to be born again, born of the Spirit.

A New Heart Also Will I Give You

Ezekiel 36:26–27A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh. And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.

God promised a new heart, a new spirit, and His own Spirit within, causing us to walk in His statutes and keep His judgments. The new birth does not do away with God's statutes. The Spirit inside is what causes us to walk in them.

A New Creature

2 Corinthians 5:17Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.

Old things are passed away. All things are become new.

Sealed With That Holy Spirit of Promise

Ephesians 1:13In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise,

The moment you believed, God sealed you with His Spirit. He set His mark on you. You are His.

B.We Have the Mind of Christ

The Mind of Christ

1 Corinthians 2:16For who hath known the mind of the Lord, that he may instruct him? But we have the mind of Christ.

This is what happens at the new birth. God does not forgive you and leave you with the old mind. He gives you the mind of Christ. If the Spirit of Christ dwells in you, you are His (Romans 8:9), and the mind of the Head now reaches every member.

Let This Mind Be in You

Philippians 2:2–5Fulfil ye my joy, that ye be likeminded, having the same love, being of one accord, of one mind. Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves. Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others. Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus:

Look at what the mind of Christ does. It is of one accord, of one mind. It does nothing through strife. It esteems others better than itself. It looks on the things of others. That is exactly what every member of your body does. The hand looks out for the foot, and the eye looks out for the hand. The mind of Christ thinks the way a healthy body works.

C.Same Members, New Orders

Instruments of Righteousness

Romans 6:13Neither yield ye your members as instruments of unrighteousness unto sin: but yield yourselves unto God, as those that are alive from the dead, and your members as instruments of righteousness unto God.

Here is the beauty of it. God does not hand you new feet when you get saved. He gives the same feet new orders. The feet that ran to mischief now carry the gospel. The mouth that cursed now blesses. The hands that took now give (Ephesians 4:28). The members are the same, but the mind is new.

The Renewing of Your Mind

Romans 12:1–2I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service. And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

Present your body, every member of it, to God. Then keep renewing your mind so the world does not shape you.

Renewed in the Spirit of Your Mind

Ephesians 4:22–24That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man, which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts; And be renewed in the spirit of your mind; And that ye put on the new man, which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness.

Put off the old man and put on the new. It starts in the spirit of your mind.

Ye Are Not Your Own

1 Corinthians 6:19–20What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own? For ye are bought with a price: therefore glorify God in your body, and in your spirit, which are God's.

Your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost, and you were bought with a price. Your members belong to God now, so glorify Him in your body.

The Mind of Christ Will Never Harm His Own Body

Christ nourisheth and cherisheth His church (Ephesians 5:29). If His mind is in you, it will never send you to wound His body. When a member is tearing down another member, it is not following the Head. It is following the old mind. The answer is not to cut that member off. It is for that member to come back under the mind of Christ.

Closing

A Body That Builds Itself Up in Love

The Edifying of Itself in Love

Ephesians 4:15–16But speaking the truth in love, may grow up into him in all things, which is the head, even Christ: From whom the whole body fitly joined together and compacted by that which every joint supplieth, according to the effectual working in the measure of every part, maketh increase of the body unto the edifying of itself in love.

The opposite of self harm is not just leaving each other alone. It is the edifying of itself in love. A healthy body builds itself up, and every joint supplies something.

Restore Such an One

Galatians 6:1Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted.

When a member is overtaken in a fault, the spiritual ones restore it gently, remembering they could fall too. Your body does not cut off a broken arm. It sets it, holds it still, and lets it heal.

By This Shall All Men Know

John 13:34–35A new commandment I give unto you, That ye love one another; as I have loved you, that ye also love one another. By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples, if ye have love one to another.

The world will know we belong to Jesus by the way we treat each other. A body that cares for its own members is a witness.

Think about what your body does when you cut your hand. Before you even think about it, the blood starts to clot and the body sends its healers to the wound. Your other hand covers it. You favor it for days until it heals. Nobody had to teach your body to do that. God built it in. That is what the body of Christ looks like with the mind of Christ in it.

Summary to Restate

  • Your body was made by God to work as one (Psalm 139:14).
  • No man ever yet hated his own flesh, and Christ will not harm His own body (Ephesians 5:29).
  • Every member matters, from the toes to the mind (1 Corinthians 12:22).
  • Every member serves the others. The feet carry, the hands feed, the eyes guide, and the ears keep the balance.
  • Every member can be turned into a weapon against the body (Proverbs 6:16–19).
  • The members follow the mind, and at the new birth God gives us the mind of Christ (1 Corinthians 2:16).
  • A body with the mind of Christ builds itself up in love (Ephesians 4:16).

The Appeal

For the believer: Ask yourself which member you are, and whether you have been helping the body or hurting it. Has your mouth been feeding the body or poisoning it? Have your ears been listening to things they should not? Have your fingers been pointing? Bring it to the altar and yield your members to God again (Romans 6:13).

For the church: Let us be a body that runs toward the hurting member instead of away from it, with the same care one for another (1 Corinthians 12:25).

For anyone not yet born again: Your members have been taking orders from the old mind. Jesus said, Ye must be born again (John 3:7). Confess with your mouth the Lord Jesus and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, and you will be saved (Romans 10:9–10). God will put His Spirit in you and give you a new mind.

For anyone hurting themselves: If you have been harming your own body, hear this. You are fearfully and wonderfully made (Psalm 139:14). Your body is not your enemy, and God does not see you as worthless. You were bought with a price (1 Corinthians 6:20). Cast all your care on Him, for He cares for you (1 Peter 5:7). Please do not carry this alone. Talk to the pastor or someone you trust today. If you are in danger right now, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.

Closing Prayer Focus

  • Thank God for the body He gave each of us, and for the body of Christ.
  • Ask Him to heal every wound His members have given one another (Galatians 6:1).
  • Ask Him to guard our mouths and close our ears to a naughty tongue (Proverbs 17:4).
  • Ask that the mind of Christ would rule in every member (Philippians 2:5).
  • Pray for any who are hurting themselves, that they would know they are loved and that they are not their own (1 Corinthians 6:19).
