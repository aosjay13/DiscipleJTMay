A Pronomian Teaching

The Armor of God

Standing Strong in the Whole Armor of God

✦

Introduction

The Christian life is not only a walk but a warfare. The believer who would follow Christ faithfully will find himself in a real conflict against a real enemy, and he is not left to fight it unarmed. In the closing words of his letter to the Ephesians, Paul calls the people of God to put on the whole armor of God, that they might stand against the wiles of the devil and hold their ground in the evil day.

This armor is not made of steel but of the graces and truths of God, each piece a part of the provision He has made for our protection and victory. Paul pictures the believer as a soldier fully equipped, every part guarded, ready to stand. We will look at the nature of the battle, then at each piece of the armor in turn, and at the prayer that holds it all together, that we may learn to stand strong in the strength of the Lord and in the power of His might.

Section I

The Battle Is Spiritual, and the Strength Is the Lord's

Before naming a single piece of armor, Paul tells us two things we must grasp. First, our strength for this battle is not our own but the Lord's. Second, our true enemy is not flesh and blood, not the people around us, but the spiritual powers of darkness. We fight in His strength, against an unseen foe, and so we need His armor and not our own resources.

Ephesians 6:10–12 Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.
2 Corinthians 10:4 For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds.

Mark these two truths well. We are to be strong in the Lord, not in ourselves, for the power belongs to Him. And we wrestle not against flesh and blood, so our weapons are not the carnal weapons of this world but the spiritual armor and the mighty power of God. When we forget either truth, fighting in our own strength or treating people as the enemy, we lose the battle before it begins.

His Strength, an Unseen Foe

The battle is spiritual and the strength is the Lord's. We are to be strong in Him, not in ourselves, and we wrestle not against flesh and blood but against the powers of darkness. Our weapons are not carnal but mighty through God.

Section II

The Belt of Truth and the Breastplate of Righteousness

The first two pieces Paul names are the belt of truth and the breastplate of righteousness. The belt held the soldier's garments together and braced his body for battle. So truth holds the believer's life together, the truth of God's word believed and lived, with no place given to falsehood. The breastplate guarded the heart and vital organs. So righteousness, the righteousness of Christ received and the righteous life that flows from it, guards the believer's heart against the accusations of the enemy.

Ephesians 6:14 Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness.
John 8:32 And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.
Isaiah 59:17 For he put on righteousness as a breastplate, and an helmet of salvation upon his head; and he put on the garments of vengeance for clothing, and was clad with zeal as a cloke.

The enemy is the father of lies, and so truth is our first defense, the honest heart that lives in the light of God's word. And the accuser charges us with our sins, so righteousness is our breastplate, both the righteousness of Christ that covers us and the righteous walk that gives the enemy no foothold. Notice from Isaiah that this is the very armor God Himself wears. We put on the armor of God because it is His own.

Truth and Righteousness Together

The belt of truth braces the believer in the honesty of God's word, giving no place to the father of lies. The breastplate of righteousness guards the heart, both the righteousness of Christ that covers us and the righteous walk that leaves the enemy no foothold to accuse.

Section III

The Shoes of the Gospel of Peace

The soldier's feet were shod with sturdy footwear that gave him a firm footing in battle. For the believer, this footwear is the preparation of the gospel of peace. The gospel that gives us peace with God also gives us a sure footing to stand, and it sends us out to carry that peace to others. We stand firm because we are at peace with God, and we move forward in the readiness the gospel gives.

Ephesians 6:15 And your feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace.
Romans 5:1 Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ.
Romans 10:15 And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!

Notice the double work of these gospel shoes. They give us a firm footing, for being justified by faith we have peace with God and cannot be shaken from that ground. And they make us ready to go, our feet beautiful as they carry the good news of peace to others. The believer stands his ground in the peace of the gospel and advances to bring that same peace to a world at war with God.

Firm Footing in the Gospel

The gospel of peace shoes the believer's feet, giving a sure footing because we have peace with God through Christ. The same gospel makes us ready to carry peace to others. We stand firm in it and move forward to share it.

Section IV

The Shield of Faith and the Helmet of Salvation

Above all, Paul says, take the shield of faith. The large shield of the soldier could cover his whole body and quench the flaming arrows of the enemy. So faith covers the believer and quenches the fiery darts the devil hurls at him, the darts of doubt, fear, temptation, and accusation. And the helmet of salvation guards the head and the mind, the assurance of salvation protecting our thoughts from despair and discouragement.

Ephesians 6:16–17 Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.
1 John 5:4 For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith.
1 Thessalonians 5:8 But let us, who are of the day, be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love; and for an helmet, the hope of salvation.

The shield of faith is set above all the rest, for faith takes God at His word against every lie and fear the enemy sends. When the fiery darts of doubt and temptation come flying, faith lifts the shield and quenches them in the promises of God. And the helmet of salvation guards the mind with the sure hope of our salvation, so that we are not cast down by discouragement. Faith and the assured hope of salvation together keep the believer steady in the fight.

Quenching the Fiery Darts

The shield of faith is set above all, for faith takes God at His word and quenches every fiery dart of doubt, fear, and temptation. The helmet of salvation guards the mind with the sure hope of salvation, keeping the believer from despair and discouragement in the battle.

Section V

The Sword of the Spirit, the Word of God

Of all the pieces, only one is a weapon of attack, the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God. The rest of the armor guards and defends, but the sword strikes. The word of God is the believer's weapon against the enemy, sharp and powerful, the very weapon the Lord Jesus Himself used to defeat the devil in the wilderness, answering every temptation with it is written.

Ephesians 6:17 And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.
Hebrews 4:12 For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.
Matthew 4:10 Then saith Jesus unto him, Get thee hence, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.

See how the Lord wielded this sword. When tempted, He did not argue in His own words but answered each assault with the written word of God, it is written, and the enemy fled. So the word of God is our sword, quick and powerful, able to cut through every lie and turn back every temptation. This is why the believer must know the Scriptures, hiding them in his heart, that he may have the sword ready in his hand when the enemy comes.

It Is Written

The word of God is the only weapon of attack in the armor, the sword of the Spirit. The Lord Jesus defeated the tempter by answering it is written. The believer must know the Scriptures and hide them in his heart, that the sword may be ready when the enemy comes.

Section VI

Praying Always: The Armor Held Together

Paul does not end with the sword. He goes on at once to prayer, for the whole armor is put on and held together by praying always in the Spirit. Prayer is the atmosphere in which the soldier of Christ lives and fights. Without it, the armor hangs idle. With it, every piece is kept in place and the believer stands watchful and strong, depending moment by moment on the Lord whose strength he fights in.

Ephesians 6:18 Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints.
Ephesians 6:13 Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand.

Notice the call to pray always, watching with perseverance, and to pray for all the saints. The battle is not fought alone but alongside all the people of God, and prayer binds us together and to the Lord. And mark the goal Paul sets, that having done all, we may stand. The aim of all the armor is to stand, to hold our ground and not be moved, until the evil day is past and the victory of Christ is fully revealed.

Having Done All, to Stand

Prayer puts on and holds together the whole armor, for the soldier of Christ fights in constant dependence on the Lord. We pray always, watching for ourselves and for all the saints. The goal of the armor is to stand, holding our ground until the evil day is past.

Conclusion

Standing Strong in the Armor of God

The Christian life is a warfare, but the believer is not left unarmed. God has provided the whole armor for our protection and victory, the belt of truth and the breastplate of righteousness, the gospel shoes that give us firm footing, the shield of faith that quenches every fiery dart, the helmet of salvation that guards the mind, and the sword of the Spirit, the word of God, our weapon against the enemy. And all of it is put on and held together by praying always in the Spirit.

We fight not in our own strength but in the strength of the Lord, not against flesh and blood but against the powers of darkness. So let us put on every piece of the armor God has given, taking up the shield of faith above all, keeping the sword of His word ready in our hands, and praying without ceasing. Then, clothed in the armor of God and standing in His might, we shall withstand in the evil day, and having done all, we shall stand.

Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.
